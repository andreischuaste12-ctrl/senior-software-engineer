# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and useful for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept reasonable correction.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and security boundaries.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.
- Help newcomers understand the technical context and contribution process.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure of
private information, sustained disruption, and knowingly misleading claims are
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories and in public spaces where someone is
representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove material, pause a
discussion, reject a contribution, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Decisions should consider severity, repetition, context, and the
safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/andreischuaste12-ctrl). Do not put private or
sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical. A maintainer who is involved in a report must not
make the enforcement decision alone.

This policy is original project documentation and may be improved through the
normal contribution process.
